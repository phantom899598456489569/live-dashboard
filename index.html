How to Use

  1. Paste a link — any YouTube watch, youtu.be, /live/, Shorts link, or just the 11-character video ID.
  2. Press Load Video — the player starts instantly and the video is saved to your history.
  3. Stop Player — stops playback and returns the status to Offline.
  4. History — click any saved thumbnail to reload that video, or remove it.
  5. Live on your channel (OBS, free): press Stream Mode — a clean full-screen page opens. Copy its link, then in OBS add a Browser Source with that link, paste your channel's stream key from YouTube Studio → Go live, and press Start Streaming in OBS. The channel is whichever stream key you paste.
  6. Direct stream link: you can also open the dashboard with ?live=1&v=VIDEO_ID at the end of the page URL — it opens straight in full-screen stream mode with that video playing. Example: index.html?live=1&v=dQw4w9WgXcQ
